# Scientific Review

1. Read workspace instructions, the domain checklist, the manuscript and any
   supplied journal requirements or reviewer reports. State inspection coverage.
2. Summarize the research question and claimed contribution without endorsing novelty.
3. Evaluate relevant model assumptions, contact/lubrication/coupling formulations,
   boundary conditions, numerical convergence, baselines, validation and uncertainty.
4. Check consistency among abstract, figures, tables, methods and conclusions.
   Separate confirmed contradictions from questions requiring author clarification.
5. Write `reviews/<name>-review.md`: overall assessment, major issues, minor issues,
   each with location, evidence, significance and a concrete revision or check.
   Prioritize by effect on conclusions; do not invent journal acceptance probabilities.
6. For reviewer responses, address every supplied point with a respectful response,
   change/location and supporting evidence. Mark proposed or unperformed analyses
   explicitly; never claim a revision, experiment or simulation was completed when not.
7. Deliver scientific feedback in Chinese by default, and manuscript wording or
   reviewer response text in English unless the user specifies otherwise.
